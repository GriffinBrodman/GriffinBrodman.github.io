Wild rooms: keep, change or cut

35 rare special rooms, now in main. Each turns on something a hero has: a hobby, a pet, a keepsake, a fear, a trait, a song they wrote, a crew bond. A delve meets about 1–3 of them; none is more than about 10% of wild-room meetings. Names in the sample lines are stand-ins (Brix, Pickles). Reply with numbers to cut or change.

1the drake's cinder bed

Cragtooth Warren, Emberdeep · also called the drowsy drake's hoard · fight

A drake sleeps on a heap of warm coins, one eye not quite shut. The cave smells of hot pennies and old smoke.

In the room: a drake asleep on a heap of warm coins, one eye not quite shut

What a crew can do

  • lullaby: sing it deeper asleep · tests heart · the best-placed hero tries · helped by: lullaby, song of rest, campfire chorus, hymn singer
  • sneak: tiptoe round the heap · tests agility · the loudest hero decides it
  • wake: wake it up and have it out · the best-placed hero tries

How it reads

  • lullaby:pass Brix hums low and slow and the drake's good eye closes. By the third verse the whole heap rises and falls in time, and the heap is picked over on tiptoe.
  • lullaby:partial Brix gets the drake nodding, but the last line wobbles. It opens one eye, groggy and sulky.
  • lullaby:fail Brix picks the wrong song. The drake lifts its head to hear it better, and then to deal with it.

In the paper

  • lullaby:pass Drake sleeps through own robbery. 'It was the third verse,' says Brix. 'Nobody stays awake for the third verse.'
  • lullaby:partial Brix sang a drake in the dungeon nearly to sleep. Nearly, the drake would like it noted, is not asleep.
  • lullaby:fail A drake in the dungeon has heard Brix sing and would like a word. Several words, mostly fire.
  • lullaby:bad Brix sang to a drake in the dungeon and hit the high note. The drake has reviewed the performance.
  • sneak:pass Brix tiptoed past a sleeping drake in the dungeon with their boots in hand. The drake slept on, none the wiser.

Margin note: Hum LOW. He likes the slow ones. Do not do the high bit.
