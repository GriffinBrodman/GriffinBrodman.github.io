Dungeon maps, second pass

The fixes from your notes, on their own branch (not merged): a fresh 90-day demo league. The isometric stack is gone (kept in an unused/ folder), floors are tabs, tapping a room shows what happened there, the map shows walked rooms plus the rooms one passage away and nothing else, and floor layouts are no longer straight lines.

1. Try it

  1. Ashveil CryptsA crypt laid out on a processional axis with mirrored side chambers. Tap any inked room: its card opens with who cleared it and when, the story, notes and a ▸ watch for that day.
  2. Thornmere RuinsA courtyard hub with broken buildings around it.
  3. Frostspire · Sunken Vault · EmberdeepThe deep ones, partly mapped: a tower of rings, a vault, a cave. Check that pencilled "?" rooms are only ever one passage off a walked one.
  4. Cragtooth WarrenA goblin warren: clusters of burrows with loops.
