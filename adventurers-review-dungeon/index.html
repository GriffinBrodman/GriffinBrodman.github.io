Dungeon module review

A demo league (20 made-up heroes, seed 11) run to the end of a 90-day age on the finished dungeon branch, before it merges. It doesn't have the road or class changes yet; those are already on main. Tap a link to open the real site at that spot.

1. The dungeon page

  1. Thornmere RuinsThe best one to start with: three floors and two boss kills. On the left is the floor stack (tap a floor). Below it are the inked floor sheet with the heroes' handwritten notes, the Spoils list, and "Days below".
  2. FrostspireFour floors of ice. Quill died on floor 3 on day 80.
  3. Sunken Vault · EmberdeepThe sea and fire themes. Neither boss falls this age.
  4. Wolf Den · Cragtooth WarrenOld Snapjaw, the famous bear trap, is in the Wolf Den. The Mimic King is in Cragtooth.

2. Watching a day underground

  1. On any dungeon page, scroll to Days below and tap ▸ watchThe crew's banner walks the map room by room. Each room opens a small scene: a trap sprung or spotted, a chest with the loot flying out, a fight, a puzzle, a camp. Then the room inks in. Does each scene show what its line says? Are the transitions smooth?

3. Dungeons in the paper

  1. Day 15The Silk Matriarch falls, and a wild room: "It was a mimic. Everybody said it was a mimic. Juniper: 'Worth it.'"
  2. Day 5A famous trap is disarmed (the Warchief's Bucket), and the first unique comes home.
  3. Day 56Brix kills Gruk the Warchief's Heir and keeps a vow.
  4. Day 68A refusal: Hazel sits down on floor 2 and offers to mind the bags.
  5. Day 80A death underground: Quill in Frostspire, maimed earlier and season 3, as the rules require.

4. Heroes

  1. SelaThe most stressed hero (she snaps on day 79). Look at the stress breakdown and the plain-word lines.
  2. VexThe most delves. Look at why the crew went down, what they packed, why they came up, and the kit in plain words.
